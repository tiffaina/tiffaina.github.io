About

I'm a Fulbright Scholar pursuing an MVA (Mathematics, Vision, and Learning) Health Track master's at ENS Paris-Saclay, working at the intersection of medical imaging, machine learning, and clinical applications.

Currently researching spine morphometrics using foundation models at AP-HP Pitié-Salpêtrière. Previously: computational neuroscience at MIT's McGovern Institute, neuropsychiatric drug discovery at Alkermes, and dual degrees in Computer Science and Neuroscience from Brown University.

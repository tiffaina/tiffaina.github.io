ABOUT

I'm a generalist drawn to creative problem-solving at the intersection of medicine and technology. What motivates me isn’t loyalty to a single field but the kinds of problems that demand inventive solutions. I’m interested in building AI that can transform diagnostics and treatment by offloading the cognitive and administrative weight that keeps physicians from doing what matters most: treating patients like people.

I'm currently a Fulbright Scholar pursuing the MVA in Mathematics, Vision, and Learning (Health Track) at ENS Paris-Saclay. I earned dual degrees in Computer Science and Neuroscience from Brown University, where I came to understand that brain science isn’t adjacent to AI—it’s the discipline that made it possible. Neural networks aren't metaphors; they're built on the same principles that govern how biological systems think, learn, and recognize patterns.

Outside tech, I fence, write prose poetry, read philosophy, and disappear into whatever psychoanalytic rabbit hole I'm currently obsessed with, usually while searching for Paris’ best vegan bakeries.

ABOUT

I'm a Fulbright Scholar pursuing an MVA (Mathematics, Vision, and Learning) Health Track master's at ENS Paris-Saclay, working at the intersection of medical imaging, machine learning, and clinical applications.

Currently researching spine morphometrics using foundation models at AP-HP Pitié-Salpêtrière Hospital in Paris. Previously: computational neuroscience at MIT's McGovern Institute, neuropsychiatric drug discovery at Alkermes, and dual degrees in Computer Science and Neuroscience from Brown University.

Beyond the lab, I fence competitively, explore Parisian opera houses, and think about how technology shapes (and is shaped by) the humans who build and use it.
